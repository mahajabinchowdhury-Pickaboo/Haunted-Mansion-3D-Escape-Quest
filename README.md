# 👻 Haunted Mansion: 3D Escape Quest

## 📖 Project Overview

**Haunted Mansion: 3D Escape Quest** is a 3D survival puzzle escape game developed using Python and OpenGL. The player wakes up trapped inside a haunted mansion filled with locked doors, hidden passages, mysterious keys, puzzle switches, and dangerous ghosts. The objective is to explore the mansion, solve puzzles, survive ghost encounters, and escape before the timer runs out.

The game combines 3D graphics, interactive environments, enemy AI, collision detection, object animation, and multiple gameplay systems to create an engaging escape-room experience.

## 🎮 Key Features

- **3D Mansion Environment:** Explore multiple connected areas, including the entrance hall, library, dining room, bedroom, basement, secret room, and exit gate.
- **Player Movement:** Navigate the mansion using keyboard controls.
- **Collision Detection:** Prevent movement through walls and other obstacles.
- **Key and Door System:** Collect keys to unlock restricted areas and progress through the game.
- **Ghost AI:** Encounter ghosts that patrol the mansion and chase the player when nearby.
- **Health System:** Manage health and avoid ghost attacks.
- **Countdown Timer:** Escape before time runs out.
- **Puzzle System:** Activate switches in the correct sequence to unlock new areas and reveal the final key.
- **Hidden Room:** Discover secret passages by activating switches and moving bookshelves.
- **3D Animations:** Experience floating ghosts, rotating keys, moving bookshelves, and animated player movement.
- **Dual Camera Modes:** Switch between first-person and third-person perspectives.
- **Difficulty Modes:** Choose Easy, Normal, or Hard difficulty.
- **Cheat Mode:** Simplify gameplay for testing and demonstration.
- **Game States:** Includes win and lose conditions, along with a restart system.
- **In-Game HUD:** View health, remaining time, collected keys, difficulty, and gameplay messages.

## 🧩 Gameplay

1. Start in the mansion's entrance hall.
2. Explore the rooms and collect the first key from the dining room.
3. Unlock the library and activate its switch to reveal a hidden passage.
4. Enter the secret room and collect the next key.
5. Unlock the basement and solve the switch puzzle in the correct order: **Red → Blue → Green**.
6. Collect the final key and unlock the exit gate.
7. Reach the escape zone before your health or time runs out.

Be careful! Ghosts can chase you, reduce your health, and become more dangerous as you progress.

## ⚙️ Difficulty Levels

| Difficulty | Time Limit | Starting Health |
|---|---:|---:|
| Easy | 240 seconds | 5 |
| Normal | 180 seconds | 3 |
| Hard | 120 seconds | 2 |

Higher difficulty increases the challenge through faster ghosts, a greater detection range, and more dangerous encounters.

## 🏆 Win and Lose Conditions

**Win:** Collect the required keys, solve the puzzles, open the final exit gate, and reach the escape zone before the timer expires.

**Lose:** The game ends when the timer reaches zero or the player's health drops to zero.
